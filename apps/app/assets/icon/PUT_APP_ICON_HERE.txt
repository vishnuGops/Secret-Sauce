Place your app launcher icon here as:  app_icon.png  (1024 x 1024 px, PNG)

Then generate platform icons with:
  melos run gen:icons
  # or, from apps/app:  dart run flutter_launcher_icons

Config lives in apps/app/pubspec.yaml under `flutter_launcher_icons`.
For an Android adaptive icon, also add app_icon_foreground.png and uncomment
the adaptive_icon_* lines in that config.
